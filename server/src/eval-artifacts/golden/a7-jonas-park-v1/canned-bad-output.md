### LinkedIn About

Coastal restoration used to wait on a perfect survey season before anyone planted a single blade of eelgrass, and I spent eight years proving that waiting is how marshes and tidal creeks disappear for good.

At Tidewatch Conservancy I learned how field crews actually ship. At NOAA Restoration Center I founded the eelgrass mapping practice that later scaled.

One delivery replanted 14 hectares of eelgrass across three bays.

If a municipal resilience team needs a clearer path from survey to planting, reach out.
